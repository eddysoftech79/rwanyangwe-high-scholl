About Our School

Rwanyangwe High School is committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined and supportive learning environment. We aim to develop students academically, morally, socially and practically so that they can become responsible and productive members of society.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence, character development and innovation, preparing learners for a successful future.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through effective teaching, discipline, innovation and a supportive learning environment that enables every learner to reach their potential.

Our Values

We promote discipline, integrity, respect, hard work, teamwork, responsibility and excellence in all aspects of school life.

Academics

Our academic programme is designed to develop strong knowledge, practical skills, creativity and critical thinking. We provide learners with a balanced education that prepares them for further studies, careers and responsible citizenship.

Sciences

We promote scientific knowledge and problem-solving through subjects such as Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics.

Humanities

Learners develop an understanding of society, history, geography, culture and values through subjects such as History, Geography and CRE.

ICT & Technology

Students develop computer literacy, digital skills and practical technology skills needed in the modern world.
